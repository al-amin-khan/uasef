# Progressive discovery interview

Do not dump the entire checklist on the user. Ask 1–4 decisive questions, record answers, revisit only when scope changes.

1. **Problem**: who feels the pain, frequency, severity, current workaround, evidence that solving it matters?
2. **Users and buyers**: primary/secondary personas, permissions, who pays, accessibility, languages, devices, offline needs?
3. **Value**: measurable user outcome, adoption threshold, business model, success and stop criteria?
4. **Core workflows**: triggers, steps, approval, failure, exceptions, abuse and conflicting actions?
5. **Data**: what is collected, sensitivity, retention, backups, residency, consent, deletion, tenancy?
6. **Integration**: existing systems, vendor APIs, authentication, payments, webhooks, external dependency limits?
7. **Scale and reliability**: registered vs active vs concurrent users, peak timings, latency, SLO and recovery goals?
8. **Team and budget**: delivery date, developer capacity, recurring budget, hosting, constraints, deployment skill?
9. **Unknowns**: classify Must-Answer-Now vs researchable vs safely deferrable; record assumption owners.
10. **Scope guard**: identify compelling alternative of not building software or using an existing product.

## Output format
Confirmed facts | hypotheses | open questions | evidence link | urgency | affected decision.
